Qualifications retained when using the slides:

- Public availability does not erase copyright or enforceable contractual restrictions.
- Passive receipt of a recruit's notes does not automatically establish misappropriation;
  connect the alleged acquisition/use/disclosure to the required knowledge.
- A prompt response to a chatbot disclosure creates no automatic secrecy safe harbor.
- The slide's statutory-damages totals are hypothetical; registration timing,
  willfulness, innocent infringement, and the number of qualifying works matter.
- Nominative-use law is framed under the Ninth Circuit's test; "no logos" is a
  conservative suggestion, rather than an absolute legal prohibition. Separately
  assess misleading factual comparisons; vague puffery may be nonactionable.
- UK Getty model-weight reasoning is not controlling U.S. law. Output filtering
  and retraining recommendations do not automatically resolve every input-copying claim.
