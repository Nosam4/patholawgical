# AI and the Law: Weeks 8–9

The combined **Weeks 8–9: Intellectual Property** subject follows
`Week8&9/Week_8-9_Readings.docx`, assigned for October 6 and 13, 2026.
It contains 15 short recall drills with 68 blanks. The four discussion questions
guide coverage: AI IP considerations, copyright and fair use, patent requirements,
and company protection through patents, copyright, trademarks, and trade secrets.

The local document contains the reading list, not the assigned ABA chapter 10 or
Garon chapter 4. Those chapters were not available for extraction. The assigned
[Norton Rose Fulbright case update](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/ce8eaa5f/ai-in-litigation-series-an-update-on-ai-copyright-cases-in-2026)
returned HTTP 403. The drills use the listed topics and cases, with the primary
authorities below supplying the rules; they do not claim complete textbook coverage.
The optional podcast is not required to complete the drills.

| Human authorship | [Copyright Office Part 2](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf), [Thaler appellate opinion](https://media.cadc.uscourts.gov/opinions/docs/2025/03/23-5233.pdf), and [Supreme Court docket 25-449](https://www.supremecourt.gov/search.aspx?filename=/docket/docketfiles/html/public/25-449.html). | Fair use; acquisition, training, outputs | [17 U.S.C. § 107](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#107) and [Copyright Office Part 3, pre-publication report](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-3-Generative-AI-Training-Report-Pre-Publication-Version.pdf). Analyze each activity separately and weigh all factors in context. |
| Compare the training cases | [ROSS opinion, February 11, 2025](https://www.ded.uscourts.gov/sites/ded/files/opinions/20-613_5.pdf), [Bartz order, June 23, 2025](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-3_24-cv-05417/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-3_24-cv-05417-0.pdf), and [Kadrey opinion, June 25, 2025](https://authorsguild.org/app/uploads/2025/06/gov.uscourts.cand_.415175.598.0_1.pdf). ROSS addressed non-generative competing legal research; Bartz separated training from pirated-library copying; Kadrey depended on the plaintiffs' evidence. |
| Bartz settlement | [Final-approval order, July 20, 2026](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/california/candce/4%3A2024cv05417/434709/680/). The $1.5 billion settlement concerns covered books on the Works List; output claims and conduct on or after August 25, 2025 are excluded from the release. |
| OpenAI MDL; Disney/Universal v. Midjourney | [JPML transfer order, April 3, 2025](https://cases.justia.com/federal/district-courts/none/jpml/3%3A2023cv03223/1705358/34/0.pdf) and [Midjourney complaint, June 11, 2025](https://docketnexus.com/document/442330410/). These drills identify the procedural device and allegations, without claiming final merits outcomes. |
| Patentability requirements | [USPTO quality metrics](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/quality-metrics) and [35 U.S.C. § 112](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/35/112). Eligibility and utility, novelty, nonobviousness, and disclosure/claim requirements are separate gates. |
| Inventorship; human conception | [Revised USPTO guidance, November 28, 2025](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/28/2025-21457/revised-inventorship-guidance-for-ai-assisted-inventions). This rescinds the February 2024 inventorship guidance. AI is a tool; apply ordinary human conception standards. Pannu still applies to joint human inventorship. |
| Subject-matter eligibility | [Assigned July 2024 update](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/07/17/2024-15377/2024-guidance-update-on-patent-subject-matter-eligibility-including-on-artificial-intelligence) and [current USPTO guidance hub](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/laws/examination-policy/subject-matter-eligibility), checked October 7, 2026. | Squires's policy statement | [Assigned Senate statement, October 9, 2025](https://www.uspto.gov/about-us/news-updates/statement-director-squires-united-states-senate-subcommittee-intellectual). The drill labels the director's positions as policy advocacy. |
| AI branding; confidential know-how | [USPTO likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion), [trade secret policy](https://www.uspto.gov/ip-policy/trade-secret-policy), and [18 U.S.C. § 1839](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title18/html/USCODE-2024-title18-partI-chap90-sec1839.htm).
