# Evidence: cumulative final review

Evidence has 82 drills and 670 recall targets across 11 subjects, reduced from
105 drills and 834 targets. The final covers the full course; earlier rules remain
part of review.

The midterm applications section and three-case midterm quiz are removed, along
with the duplicate full analysis checklist. The eight-step analysis checklist
remains the starting point. Its scope notes now include later-course topics
such as hearsay, privileges, and confrontation.

The practice menu retains:

- All 51 existing individual FRE rule drills, including Rule 804 in RANTED order.
- Rule-name memorization and all four courtroom objection-script drills.
- All 13 final-exam cases and the complete case-identification quiz.
- Eight hearsay-element drills and the five confrontation principles.
- Witness foundations in **Privileges & Witnesses**, and illustrative aids versus
  summaries in **Authentication & Contents**. These two drills preserve foundational
  topics previously available only in the removed applications section.

The two moved drills retain their original IDs and answers. Other retained drills
also retain their progress signatures, so existing scores and unfinished runs
continue to work. Removed drills no longer appear in the menu, random practice,
or saved-run suggestions. Browser storage is not cleared.
