# Evidence: Hearsay Exception Elements

Evidence → **Hearsay Exception Elements** contains eight drills with 27 recall
targets. Seven use the existing list-recall interface: enter each item in any
order, reveal omissions, and practice missed items. The eighth uses short
application questions for hearsay within hearsay. New identifiers keep these
scores separate from the existing FRE 803 exception-name drill.

## Rule text and clarifications

Checked October 29, 2026 against the official
[Federal Rules of Evidence, December 1, 2025 compilation](https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-evidence.pdf),
printed pages 18–19 (803) and 23 (805). Each drill links to the relevant PDF page.

- The six 803(5) targets preserve the professor's order: once had personal
  knowledge; has forgotten; record exists; made or adopted; while fresh;
  testifies that the record was accurate. The explanation clarifies that the
  witness need only lack sufficient recollection to testify fully and accurately.
- 803(5) permits reading the record into evidence. Only the adverse party may
  offer it as an exhibit. This limit appears after completion or reveal alongside
  the distinction from refreshing memory under 612.
- The business-records foundation includes information transmitted by someone
  with knowledge, as well as records made by that person. The opponent bears
  the burden of showing untrustworthiness under 803(6)(E); the professor's fourth
  step addresses rebutting such a showing.
- An embedded outsider statement needs an independent admissible route when
  offered for its truth. The outer business-record exception does not itself
  resolve every inner statement. A notice purpose can make the inner statement
  nonhearsay while the outer report still requires separate analysis.

Short answer aliases serve as recall labels. Full displayed answers and
post-reveal explanations retain qualifications such as the will limitation,
medical pertinence, freshness, exhibit restrictions, and the trustworthiness
burden.
