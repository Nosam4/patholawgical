## Scope

The patent drills follow Keystone Cognition's TetherTune hypothetical through
inventorship and assignments, § 101 eligibility, §§ 102–103 prior art and
nonobviousness, § 112 disclosure, enforcement, patent versus trade secret
strategy, and the conditional filing decision.

The launch drills follow Meridian Labs' Atlas 2 hypothetical through the GC
playbook, copyright's statutory order and fair-use cases, remedies, trade
secret protection and misappropriation, API contracts, trademark confusion,
and the conditions required before launch.

The invented companies, performance figures, risk ratings, and go/no-go
recommendations are course applications. They are not findings about real
companies or universal legal requirements. Activities and presentation prompts
inside the decks guide study coverage; they are not instructions to perform
those activities or contact anyone.

This review covers the supplied decks. It does not claim to reproduce textbook
chapters or other readings that were not supplied with this request.

## Clarifications in the drills

- The inventorship drill follows the November 2025 conception guidance and
  labels the February 2024 AI-specific approach as rescinded. Pannu still applies
  to joint inventorship among humans.
- The Dental Monitoring drill identifies the official opinion as
  **nonprecedential**, a detail omitted from the deck.
- Patent publication and term figures are general rules with exceptions. The
  hybrid strategy cannot withhold details required by § 112.
- Copyright exposure calculations depend on distinct eligible works, registration
  timing, proven infringement, and the available damages rules. The deck's
  figures are arithmetic illustrations rather than automatic awards.
- Fair-use results remain tied to the use and record. UK Getty is identified as
  foreign law; dated case accounts that could not be independently confirmed
  remain expressly attributed to the deck.
