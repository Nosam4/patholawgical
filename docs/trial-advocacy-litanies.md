# Trial Advocacy litanies

Open **Trial Advocacy → Litanies (Courtroom Scripts)**, beside Final Trial.
The six drills rehearse spoken lines with `BLANK` standing for changing facts.
Directions remain visible and do not count toward the score. Each line is graded
in its selected blank, and the script sections stack vertically for rehearsal.

| Drill | Scored lines | Source |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Admitting Evidence | 9 | Personal rehearsal script; admission handout for context |
| Refreshing Recollection | 8 | Personal rehearsal script; refreshing handout for context |
| Impeachments (The Three C's) | 14 | Personal rehearsal script; impeachment handout p. 1 |
| Refreshing Recollection on Cross | 9 | Refreshing handout p. 1 |
| Impeachment: Witness Asks to See | 4 | Impeachment handout p. 1 |
| Impeachment by Omission | 22 | Impeachment handout pp. 2–3 |

The original 31 spoken lines and their answer/question IDs are preserved from
`Trial Advocacy Litanies.docx`. Those lines retain the personal script's wording
and order, including “What is it?” before “How do you recognize BLANK?” in the
admission drill. New visible directions explain handoff, reading, retrieval, and
the use of conditional branches.

The additional sequences come from these local course handouts under
`TrialAdvocacy/ClassFiles/Litanies/`:

- `Admitting Exhibits Litany.pdf`, pp. 1–4.
- `Refreshing Recollection Litany.pdf`, p. 1.
- `Impeachment Litanies.pdf`, pp. 1–3.

The omission drill includes alternate responses for practice; they are not all
spoken in a single examination. Its visible directions identify when to stop,
when to show the statement, and which responses depend on the witness's answer.
The deposition-specific review language is identified as such.

The separate **Litanies** subject continues to cover the eight existing steps,
foundations, and courtroom-sequence drills. These source-based rehearsal scripts
do not replace those conceptual drills. Source documents stay local; the app
includes source names and page references rather than copies of the files.
