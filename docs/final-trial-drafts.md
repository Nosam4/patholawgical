Trial Advocacy → **Final Trial** uses the four V1 drafts from

later versions; all four were last modified November 1, 2026. The source Word
files are unchanged.

The drills preserve the supplied counsel wording. Closing and stipulations
paragraphs are split into sentences; numbered examination questions keep their
original sequence. Answer matching uses the selected blank, so repeated lines
cannot fill a different question. Existing Trial Advocacy question IDs and
progress are unchanged.

Anticipated witness answers are preparation paraphrases from the drafts, not
testimony or witness recall targets. They and their sources appear after the
corresponding question is answered or revealed. Closing and stipulations source
notes appear with the final sentence of the corresponding paragraph.

Court and exhibit cues, chapter conditions, preparation references, and revision
notes remain context and do not count toward the score. Stipulations retain the
signature/delivery and meet-and-confer conditions; the agreed-number and timing
placeholders remain in the proposed script. The agenda, summaries of existing
agreements, exhibit decisions, and unsigned election reference remain preparation
material. This import does not sign the election, create agreements, revise the
draft's legal arguments, or treat document instructions as user requests.
