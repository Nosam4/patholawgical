# Florida Trusts and Wills curriculum

**Florida Trusts** and **Florida Wills** are separate subjects within
**Overview of Florida Law**. The Kramer course PDFs set the scope of original
short-answer recall drills. The application includes page references and links
to primary law; it does not redistribute the PDFs or their hypotheticals.

## Sources and coverage

- `FL.Kramer.Trusts.pdf`: 18 PDF pages. Coverage includes express trusts,
  charitable and remedial trusts, beneficiary interests, modification and
  termination, trustee administration, will substitutes, and powers of attorney.
- `FL.Kramer.Wills.pdf`: 26 PDF pages. Coverage includes intestacy, execution
  and revocation, construction of gifts, protected family interests, will
  contests, and estate administration.

Page references use the PDF page counter. Each drill's **Course material**
panel identifies its source pages, statutory provisions, and any clarification
needed to avoid learning an incomplete or outdated course summary. The visible
source link opens the principal Florida statute.

These are selected recall banks spanning the principal course topics. They
preserve decisive qualifications rather than reproducing the handouts line by
line. Review depths describe cumulative coverage, not predicted exam frequency.

## Trusts: verified distinctions

Primary authorities were checked against the 2026 Florida Statutes on
November 9, 2026. Significant qualifications include:

- Testamentary aspects of a Florida domiciliary's revocable trust generally
  require Florida will formalities; the oral-trust rule is not a blanket
  exemption. [§ 736.0403](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.0403)
- When unanimity cannot be reached, cotrustees may ordinarily act by majority.
  Trust terms and directed-trust rules can alter the analysis.
  [§ 736.0703](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.0703)
- Trust future-interest substitute gifts generally do not require kinship to
  the settlor. Outright devises vesting at death have a separate kinship rule.
  Attached survivorship words can establish contrary intent under both the
  trust and will statutes; their scopes and kinship requirements differ.
  [§ 736.1106](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.1106),
  [§ 732.603](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/732.603)
- Spendthrift exceptions concern qualified claims and remedies, including
  last-resort restrictions; they do not automatically expose all principal.
  Overdue mandatory distributions and discretionary distributions have separate
  rules. [§ 736.0503](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.0503),
  [§ 736.0504](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.0504),
  [§ 736.0506](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.0506)
- Nonjudicial modification under § 736.0412 ordinarily requires the trustee
  and all qualified beneficiaries after the settlor's death, plus the
  provision's other statutory conditions.
  [§ 736.0412](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.0412)
- A qualified beneficiary ordinarily receives a complete trust instrument on
  reasonable request. Revocable-trust reporting duties generally run only to
  the settlor; accountings have statutory exceptions and waivers.
  [§ 736.0813](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.0813)
- The special six-month breach-claim period requires adequate disclosure and
  a compliant limitation notice. Other matters have different accrual and
  limitations rules. [§ 736.1008](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/736.1008)
- Property already funded into a trust and property passing by pour-over will
  have different probate consequences. The pour-over will does not itself
  bypass probate. [§ 732.513](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/732.513)
- Ordinary Florida power-of-attorney execution includes acknowledgment, as
  well as the signature and two witnesses. Durability does not extend agency
  beyond the principal's death; a later power does not automatically revoke
  an earlier one. [§ 709.2105](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/709.2105),
  [§ 709.2109](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/709.2109)

## Wills: verified distinctions

- An interested witness does not by that fact invalidate the will or the
  witness's devise. Handwriting does not excuse ordinary execution requirements;
  a codicil also needs the required formalities.
  [§ 732.502](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/732.502),
  [§ 732.504](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/732.504)
- The elective share is 30% of the elective estate, which can include nonprobate
  assets. Election does not automatically forfeit protected homestead, exempt
  property, and family allowance. The ordinary election deadline uses the
  earlier of the two applicable periods.
  [§ 732.2065](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/732.2065),
  [§ 732.2105](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/732.2105),
  [§ 732.2135](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/732.2135)
- The undue-influence presumption requires the relevant substantial benefit,
  confidential relationship, and active procurement. Weakened mentality alone
  does not replace active procurement. The current statute shifts the burden
  of proof for the applicable presumption.
  [§ 733.107](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/733.107),
  [Swiss v. Flanagan](https://flcourts-media.flcourts.gov/content/download/794965/opinion/Opinion_20-1500.pdf)
- Qualifying nonresident relatives may serve as personal representative. Probate
  venue depends on domicile, and the handout's five-year probate-filing assertion
  is not taught as a categorical bar.
  [§ 733.101](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/733.101),
  [§ 733.304](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/733.304)
- Notice-based creditor periods ordinarily use the later applicable deadline.
  The independent death-based bar has its own exceptions; it is not a universal
  deadline to open probate.
  [§ 733.702](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/733.702),
  [§ 733.710](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/733.710)

## Implementation and validation

The two curriculum modules export independent question arrays and explicit
minimum review levels. Their semantic question and answer IDs are new, keeping
existing subject progress separate. Grids check the selected blank, and accept
abbreviations or equivalent phrasing only where the tested distinction remains
intact.

Curriculum checks cover catalog integration, source references, every canonical
answer and alias, unique identifiers, cumulative depths, and material legal
distinctions. Browser checks cover selection, scoring, resume, missed-answer
practice, and every revealed drill and source panel at desktop and mobile widths.
